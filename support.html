AQUAJOY Support

Need help with water tracking, reminders, or AQUAJOY Premium?

Restore a purchase

Open AQUAJOY, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Manage an annual subscription

You can view, manage, or cancel an annual subscription from your Apple Account subscription settings.

Reminders are not appearing

Check that reminders are enabled in AQUAJOY and that notifications are allowed for AQUAJOY in iOS Settings. Reminders pause for the current day after you reach your hydration goal.

Contact

Email bemartinslabs@gmail.com. Include a short description of the issue, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. Do not send payment-card information.
