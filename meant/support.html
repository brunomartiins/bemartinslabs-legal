Support

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Need help with Meant? Start here. If this doesn’t cover it, email us and a real person will reply.

Restore a purchase

Open Meant, go to You and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Manage or cancel your subscription

Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Meant, or tap Manage subscription in Meant. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and a free trial converts unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reminders aren’t arriving

Check that reminders are on in Meant (You → Reminders) and that notifications are allowed for Meant in iOS Settings. Focus modes and Scheduled Summary can also hold them back. Reminders only arrive inside the time window you chose.

Adding a widget

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize, then Add Widget and choose Meant. You → Widgets in the app shows each step.

Your data

Meant has no account. Your name, choices and saved affirmations live on your iPhone, so there is no server copy to recover if the app is deleted. Restoring purchases brings back Premium access, not local content. See the Privacy Policy.
