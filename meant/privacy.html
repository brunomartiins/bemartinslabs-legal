Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Meant is an affirmations app for iPhone. This policy explains what information is handled when you use Meant, where it stays, and who else processes it. In short: your answers, name and saved affirmations stay on your iPhone. Apple and RevenueCat process subscription information.

No account

Meant has no sign-up, login or account system, and we do not run our own servers for the app.

Information stored on your device

Meant stores the following locally on your iPhone:

  • The name you choose to enter, if any.
  • Your onboarding choices, such as the desires, identity and feelings you select, and your content preferences.
  • Saved affirmations, your streak, reading position, reminder settings and appearance choice.

This information is not sent to us, to RevenueCat or to any AI service. Meant matches affirmations to your choices on the device, without runtime AI. Deleting the app normally deletes this information, unless your device or iCloud backup keeps a copy. Meant does not provide its own cloud sync.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, Meant asks iOS for notification permission and schedules them locally on your iPhone. No remote notification service is used. Reminder text is a public affirmation and does not include your name or private answers. You can change or turn off reminders in Meant or in iOS Settings.

Widgets

Widgets read dated public affirmations from a shared container on your iPhone. Your name, private choices and reflections are not stored in that container.

Sharing and Photos

When you share an affirmation, Meant creates an image and opens the iOS share sheet; you decide where it goes. Your name and private reflections are not included in the image. Saving an image asks for permission to add to your Photos. Meant can add images but cannot read your photo library.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Meant offers an optional Premium subscription. Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment-card details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to validate purchases, restore access, manage Premium access, prevent fraud and understand aggregate purchase performance.

RevenueCat may process:

  • An automatically generated anonymous app user ID.
  • Purchase and subscription information, including Apple transaction data.
  • Technical information such as device model, operating system, app version, country/storefront and last-seen time.

We do not send your name, onboarding answers, saved affirmations or any other content from the app to RevenueCat. RevenueCat handles information under its own Privacy Policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Meant contains no advertising, no third-party analytics SDK and no tracking across apps or websites. We do not access your contacts, calendar, location or advertising identifier. Apple may provide us with aggregated App Store and crash statistics if you have chosen to share them with developers in iOS Settings.

How information is used

  • To show affirmations, Topics, Moments, reminders and widgets that match your choices, on your device.
  • To process, validate and restore Premium purchases and decide whether Premium is available.
  • To prevent purchase fraud and troubleshoot purchase problems.
  • To answer you when you contact support.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information and do not use it for advertising. Purchase and technical information is shared with Apple and RevenueCat only as needed to operate subscriptions, or when required by law.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply and keep it only as long as needed to help you. The app never sends anything to us automatically.

Retention and deletion

Information on your device stays there until you delete it or delete the app. Apple and RevenueCat may retain purchase records to validate purchases, prevent fraud, comply with law and meet their own obligations. You can contact us about a privacy or deletion request at the address below; some transaction records cannot be deleted where retention is legally required.

Children

Meant is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can address it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EU, UK, Brazil (LGPD) and California, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of personal information about you. Because most Meant data never leaves your device, you control it there directly. For anything processed by RevenueCat, contact us and we will help. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings and restore purchases from within Meant.

International processing

Apple and RevenueCat may process information in countries other than the one where you live, under their policies and applicable safeguards.

Changes

We may update this policy when Meant changes or when the law requires. The date at the top shows the latest version.
